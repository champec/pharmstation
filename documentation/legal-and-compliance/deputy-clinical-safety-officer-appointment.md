## 1. Purpose

This document formally appoints a **Deputy Clinical Safety Officer (Deputy CSO)** for Pharmstation, in accordance with **NHS Digital Standard DCB0129** and the Clinical Risk Management Plan (`PS-CRMP-001`).

The Deputy CSO acts as the named backup to the substantive CSO (`PS-CSO-001`) for the purpose of business continuity. They are not involved in day-to-day clinical safety activity unless the substantive CSO is unavailable, has resigned the role, or has lost eligibility (e.g. lapse of professional registration).

This appointment closes the residual organisational risk recorded as **HAZ-016** in `PS-HAZLOG-001` (single-person sole-trader manufacturer).

## 4. Scope of Authority and Responsibilities

The Deputy CSO is granted the authority and responsibilities of the substantive CSO (per `PS-CSO-001 §4` and `PS-CRMP-001`) **only when acting** in that role.

### 4.1 Triggers for active acting

The Deputy CSO becomes the acting CSO when **any** of the following occurs:

1. The substantive CSO is unavailable for **more than 14 consecutive days** (e.g. illness, leave, incapacity).
2. The substantive CSO resigns the role with notice.
3. The substantive CSO ceases to be eligible (e.g. GPhC registration lapses, restriction is imposed).
4. A clinical safety decision is required and the substantive CSO has a declared conflict of interest.

### 4.2 Responsibilities while acting

When acting, the Deputy CSO:

- May approve or reject product releases on clinical-safety grounds.
- May issue field-safety notices to deploying pharmacies.
- Must update the Hazard Log and Clinical Safety Case Report as required.
- Must liaise with NHS England and deploying organisations on clinical safety matters.
- Must keep written records of decisions taken while acting.

### 4.3 Responsibilities while not acting

While the substantive CSO is in post and available, the Deputy CSO:

- Receives a copy of each major release CSCR for awareness.
- Is consulted (but not required to approve) where the substantive CSO seeks a second clinical opinion.
- Reviews this appointment annually with the substantive CSO.

There is **no expectation** that the Deputy CSO undertakes day-to-day clinical safety work for Pharmstation while the substantive CSO is in post.

---

## 5. Notice & Handover

- The substantive CSO will give the Deputy CSO **at least 5 working days' notice** of any planned absence longer than 14 days.
- For unplanned absences, Pharmstation senior management will notify the Deputy CSO as soon as reasonably practicable.
- A handover pack (current CSCR, open hazards, pending decisions) will be provided at the start of any acting period.
- The Deputy CSO may decline to act on any specific decision where they reasonably believe they lack the information or expertise; in that case Pharmstation senior management must appoint an alternative qualified clinician.

---

## 6. Term

This appointment is effective **30 April 2026** and remains in force until:

- The Deputy CSO resigns the role with at least **30 days' written notice**, or
- Pharmstation senior management revokes the appointment in writing, or
- The Deputy CSO ceases to be eligible (e.g. lapse of GPhC registration), in which case the appointment lapses immediately.

The substantive CSO will verify the Deputy CSO's GPhC registration status at least annually, with the next check due on or before **31 October 2026** (alignment with the deputy's GPhC renewal date).

A formal review of this appointment is scheduled for **30 April 2027** or sooner if material change occurs.

---

## 7. Indemnity & Remuneration

This is a **non-remunerated backup appointment** unless and until the Deputy CSO is required to act, at which point Pharmstation will agree reasonable compensation in writing for time worked.

The Deputy CSO is not required to take on personal liability beyond that arising from their own GPhC professional duties when exercising clinical judgement on Pharmstation's behalf. Pharmstation maintains, or will maintain before first live deployment, appropriate professional indemnity insurance covering the Deputy CSO when acting.
